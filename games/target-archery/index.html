How to Play

🏹 Aim & Shoot!

Drag anywhere on screen to aim your bow. The farther you drag, the more power! Release to shoot.

🎯 Hit the Rings!

  • Bullseye — 100 points!
  • Red Ring — 50 points
  • Blue Ring — 25 points
  • White Ring — 10 points

⬆️ Levels

Targets move faster each level! You get a few arrows per round — make them count!

Tip: Drag down and away from the target to power up your shot!
