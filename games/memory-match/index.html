How to Play

🃏 Flip two cards

Tap a card to peek. Tap a second card. Are they a pair?

✨ Match them up

Matching pairs stay face-up. Wrong pairs flip back — try to remember!

🏆 Beat your best

Every two-card try is one move. Finish with fewer moves to set a new best.

📏 Levels

Easy is 3×4, Medium is 4×4, Hard is 4×5. Bigger boards, more pairs!

Tip: remember where each animal and fruit lives. Big taps, no rushing!
