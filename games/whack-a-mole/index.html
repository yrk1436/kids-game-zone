How to Play

🔨 Bonk the Moles!

Tap moles as they pop up. Miss 5 and it's game over!

⚡ Combos

Quick consecutive bonks build a multiplier: x2, x3, x4, x5! Miss or tap wrong and it resets.

✨ Special Moles

  • 🐹 Normal Mole — 10 points
  • 🐹 Golden Mole — 5x points!
  • 🪖 Helmet Mole — Needs 2 taps
  • 💖 Heart Mole — Gives +1 life
  • ❄️ Freeze Mole — Slows moles for 5s
  • 🐰 Bunny (Decoy!) — DON'T tap! Costs a life
